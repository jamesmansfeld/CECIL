CECIL (Chicken Egg Collection Internet-Free Log) is a simple,
privacy-focused Android app for recording eggs collected from
your chickens.

FEATURES

• Record multiple egg collection sessions per day.
• Automatically use your device's local date.
• View daily egg counts in week, month and year calendar views.
• Track your all-time egg collection total.
• Use dark mode for comfortable viewing.
• Keep records stored locally on your device.

CECIL is designed to work entirely offline. It requires no
internet connection and no account.

CECIL is free and open-source software licensed under
GPL-3.0-or-later.
